Faculty Attendance Guide

Official check-in & check-out procedures
1
Start Camera & Scan Time-In
Click Start Camera and aim your lens at the rotating QR code on the Admin Station display. Your shift start will immediately log as Present (or Late if after cutoff).
2
Scan Again to Record Time-Out
After finishing your scheduled classes and campus duty, scan the Admin Station QR code once more. The system automatically registers your departure under Time-Out.
3
Direct Routing to Attendance Logs
Every scan automatically updates the Registrar's ledger and routes directly to your My Attendance Log as well as the Admin Teacher Attendance Log for real-time institutional monitoring.
4
Academic HR System Integration
Your official Time-In and Time-Out stream directly into the Academic HR System (SMS 1) for verified teaching hours, faculty duty tracking, and automated payroll processing.
Faculty Guidelines & HR Integration Notes
  • HR Direct Feed: Verified Time-In and Time-Out data feed directly into the Academic HR System without manual timesheet submission.
  • Dual Mirroring: All check-ins and check-outs are visible in your personal My Attendance Log and the Admin Teacher Attendance Log simultaneously.
  • Anti-Passback: A 5-minute cooldown protects against accidental duplicate scans.
  • Hardware Fallback: If camera is unavailable, tap your physical RFID faculty card on any campus entrance terminal.
