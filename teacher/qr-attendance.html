How to use

  1. Click Start Camera to activate your device camera.
  2. Hold the Admin-generated QR code in front of the camera within the blue guide box.
  3. The system will instantly record your Time-In.
  4. Scan again at the end of the day to record your Time-Out.
  5. Your attendance will immediately appear in My Attendance Log.
