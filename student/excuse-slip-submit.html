Submit Digital Excuse Slip

Upload proof and request excuse for unavoidable absences

Absence Request Details

Requests are reviewed by your assigned section teacher and recorded in the SMS 1 database.

Supported: PDF, JPG, PNG up to 5MB. Stored securely in campus storage bucket.

Guidelines for Excuses

  • Slips must be filed within 48 hours of returning to campus.
  • Medical excuses exceeding 1 day require a valid medical certificate from an accredited clinic.
  • Approved excuse slips adjust your status from Absent to Excused and do not penalize your 85% requirement.
  • Teachers verify excuses directly in their Section Review Workbench.
Need Immediate Help?

For prolonged hospitalization or emergencies, have your parent or guardian notify the Registrar's Office directly.
